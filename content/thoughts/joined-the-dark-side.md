So, i've joined the dark-side of you know what it is. The agentic development. Still in the state of disgust.

Been doing a lot of projects recently with opencode with the model now settling down on xiaomi/mimo-v2. Tbh, i didn't expect myself to do this much (not me, the agent), yet we've (me and mimo) produced number of projects i wouldn't be able to accomplish myself in years, in just couple of weeks.

- https://bajet.app / bajet.app/home-horizon
- https://github.com/rosengate/exedra-ts (typescript/express port of rosengate/exedra)
- some random stuff like https://github.com/eimihar/design-aspects
- and some work stuff

i realise i've enjoyed myself more on exploring ideas, and a lot what-ifs against some of these recently. this is what i felt large language are personally good for. to making use the essence made of collective human thoughts and brute force it against understanding, in making sense of things and taking it further.

but it's such a pandora-box, i think i still refuse to say that we'd all be replaced by it. writing, creating music, making software, in understanding, in reasoning. it's such an ugly way to look at the world. there're such a beauty to take it slow, and see all the underlyings, through our own hands and eyes.

to be honest, i am still mostly reading what the agent is spewing their thoughts, and still can't help having to do a lot of steering and giving them a breath of direction which i think still a novel use of ai.
